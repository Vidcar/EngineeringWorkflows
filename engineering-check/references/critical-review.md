# Critical review

Freeze or identify the exact artifact and its relevant context. Give a fresh reviewer the requirements and a neutral question, not the author's conclusion; do not hide constraints or known evidence. Focus on failure modes appropriate to the work: omitted requirements, invalid assumptions, failure and recovery, interfaces, migration, security, concurrency, accessibility, and maintenance. Review a plan as a plan, not as a reproduced runtime failure.

Preserve supported minority findings. Investigate each material disagreement with code, an independent example, or a safe discriminating probe; explain an accepted tradeoff. When the artifact changes, refresh affected review and evidence. A reviewer can return findings without being a separate model family; describe actual independence and any unavailable required coverage honestly.
