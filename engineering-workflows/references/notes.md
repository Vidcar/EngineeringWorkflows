# Write clearly and keep useful lessons

Lead technical prose with the result and why it matters. Preserve exact claims,
evidence, uncertainty and useful rationale. Cut filler and repeated narration
without making an unverified claim sound stronger. An editing request stays
within the requested text; code cleanup belongs to the implementation task.

Capture a lesson when evidence shows a reusable failure or discovery. Choose the
smallest existing mechanism that prevents recurrence: clearer ownership, a check,
a test, a helper or a short instruction with an example. Check that it catches
the failure and allows a legitimate case.

Keep the lesson once, with its scope and evidence. A single preference is not a
universal rule. Avoid permanent logs of routine activity or changes to unrelated
skills and settings. A retrospective-only request ends with findings and a
concrete proposal.
